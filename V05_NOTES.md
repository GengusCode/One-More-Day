## Interaction changes

- Relationships are kept inside an expandable panel to reduce screen clutter.
- Choice cards have clearer hover, press, and directional feedback.
- Workplace buttons no longer disclose numerical rewards, warnings, or dismissal risks before selection.
- Consequences are explained immediately after a workplace decision.
- Quiet boss favour can affect a promotion review, including through flattering or overly agreeable behaviour.
- The player name field accepts up to 32 characters.
- Leaving the name field empty selects from a diverse South African-inspired name pool.

## Design references

- Apple game onboarding guidance: get players into the core loop quickly and teach mechanics contextually.
- Apple discoverable-design guidance: controls should make their purpose clear through familiar language and visual feedback.
- Choice-driven life simulators: decisions are more interesting when the complete outcome is not known beforehand.
- Management games: repetitive income can be automated while occasional strategic decisions remain interactive.
