# ONE MORE DAY — v0.10 Level Up playtest checklist

1. Start two new lives with different names and genders. Confirm each gets four generated starter people and begins with the school test.
2. Finish one school test with a weak result. Open Study, complete the Foundation Bridge, and confirm stronger routes become reachable instead of the life becoming stuck.
3. Switch Study between **Recommended** and **View all 10 routes**. Confirm locked cards explain a practical next step and no card is cut off.
4. Try every funding method across separate lives: personal payment, bursary, study loan, current work, part-time work, and paid learnership.
5. Reload immediately before and after enrolment. Confirm the fee, stipend, part-time earnings, and daily study settlement never happen twice for the same day.
6. Try a paid learnership while employed. Cancel the warning once and confirm nothing changes; accept once and confirm the current work commitment ends before enrolment.
7. Study while working. Check that income continues, pressure/energy changes make sense, and the Study app shows progress plus the next checkpoint.
8. Complete strategy, pressure, and assessment choices. Test a pass, a rewrite, and an incomplete result; reload at each checkpoint and confirm the same programme returns.
9. Take a study loan, pass or withdraw, advance to repayments, and confirm the Life résumé shows debt, repayments, and arrears without duplicate charges.
10. Browse openings in all five career families: Trades, Technology, Business, Hospitality, and Community. Confirm each ladder contains believable progression.
11. Apply for a role, wait for the reply, and play the interview. Force one rejection and confirm that opening cools down while other families can still appear.
12. Build a strong work record, reach a promotion panel, and try different answers. Confirm promotion is influenced by performance, attendance, warnings, relationships, qualifications, employer culture, and chance.
13. Make several bad work choices. Confirm one mistake is not always a formal warning: outcomes may be no action, performance loss, verbal warning, written warning, or stronger action for serious/repeated behaviour.
14. Load a real v0.9 save. Confirm work/business level, warning history, assets, vehicles, money, relationships, age, and events survive; no qualification or study loan should appear by itself.
15. Ignore Study and start an owner-operated business. Buy an upgrade once, hire staff, expand premises, and confirm the unchanged business path still works.
16. Use **One week**, **One month**, and **Go to study checkpoint**. Confirm each stops for important decisions and no salary, stipend, business, driver, or loan movement appears twice.
17. Trigger the phone-theft chase. Win once, hit an obstacle once, pause/switch tabs, and confirm the chase remains playable with no duplicate reward.
18. Open the phone and visit Jobs, Study, Transport, Business, People, Life, and Time. From Life open Bank and Betway, then return; back and close controls must always work.
19. Test at 320 px wide, phone landscape, desktop, and 200% zoom. Check the launcher, Study cards, résumé, decisions, runner, and close buttons for clipping or horizontal scrolling.
20. Test keyboard-only navigation and reduced-motion mode. Every action needs a visible focus state, readable label, and usable no-motion fallback.
