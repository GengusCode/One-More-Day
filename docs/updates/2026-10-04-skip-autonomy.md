# Skip autonomy and visible text — 4 October 2026

Skipped weekdays now rotate and resolve ordinary work decisions through the existing career/business systems. Auto mode chooses a non-risk option that does not require discretionary spending or damage reputation. If no such option exists, it pauses at the decision. Automatic choices and their outcomes appear in the saved skip summary.

Routine reports generated by automatic choices resolve without pausing; manually scheduled important events, planned commitments, birthdays, job changes, critical health and financial pressure still interrupt. Summaries show the requested period, days actually passed and days remaining. Selected available personal transport is used instead of ignoring it in favour of taxis.

The situation panel is navy with bright white text and a gold heading. The morning plan remains gold. The production stylesheet URL is versioned to request fresh CSS on reload.

Inspiration: BitLife's automatic saving when aging and The Sims' autonomy around everyday needs. This update uses the existing event engine and does not add a new full life simulation.
