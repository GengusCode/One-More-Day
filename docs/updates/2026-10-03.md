# Staff and management — 3 October 2026

- Skilled workers are now available from Business: R150 per operating day and +2 capacity.
- Established businesses can hire one manager: requires commercial premises, at least three existing staff and a free staff slot; wage R180 per operating day.
- Business shows a separate row for each staff member and total operating payroll.
- When the owner stays home, a staffed business settles actual sales minus supplies, wages and overhead. A manager maintains more sales capacity, without guaranteeing profit.
- Home-day receipts settle once; saved staff and manager roles persist.

This replaces the old flat staff-run absence payment, which ignored running costs. Dedicated management story events remain future work.

Validation: 32/32 test files pass, including manager eligibility, duplicate prevention, payroll, absence receipts, save/load and staff-row rendering.
