# Full-period skipping — 4 October 2026

Week, month and year skips now complete 7, 30 and 365 days. Promotions, birthdays, scheduled follow-ups and choices no longer stop the requested period. Interrupted days finish through normal choice handlers, preferring affordable lower-risk options; decisions and milestones are logged in the report. No new job/business path is chosen automatically. If a life ends, the skip stops at that ending.

Repeated outcome text is excluded from the news panel, previous-outcome block and phone preview when it already appears in the current card.
