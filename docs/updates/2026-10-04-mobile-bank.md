# Compact stats and banking — 4 October 2026

- Seven stacked meters replace the large stat tiles: energy, health, happiness, knowledge, social, reputation and luck. Each has an icon and percentage.
- Changed stats display signed gains/losses that shrink and fade into the fixed percentage. Reduced motion uses a simple fade.
- Stronger borders, labels and backgrounds separate the current situation, previous outcome, follow-ups, money receipt and skip summary.
- The new Bank phone app displays current cash and separate income/expense lists, including business cost breakdowns and days.
- Newly recorded transactions are retained through saving and reloading instead of discarding all but 60. Previously discarded entries cannot be reconstructed.
- Fast-forward retains its simulation behavior. The Time app explains work, bills, wellbeing and aging; after a skip a before/after summary shows age, cash and changed stats.

No changes to save schema or income, expenses, aging or stat balance.
