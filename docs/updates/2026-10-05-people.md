# People: daily contacts

The phone’s People app now offers two interactions for existing contacts:

- Check in: 2 energy, +2 relationship, +1 social and +1 happiness.
- Catch up: 8 energy, +6 relationship, +3 social and +2 happiness.

Each person can be contacted once per calendar day. Contact is unavailable during the school finale, after life ends, or when energy is insufficient. Buttons show costs and unavailable reasons. Relationship labels and the latest reply appear on each compact contact card. Catch-up replies vary for mentors, family and other contacts; daily variation prevents the exact same reply appearing every day.

Cooldown and latest reply survive saving and loading existing v0.9 games. Interactions do not spend cash or advance the calendar. Time skipping continues using the existing daily simulation; it does not automatically contact everyone.

Validation: all 38 test files passed, including costs, input immutability, saved cooldown and reply, next-day availability, invalid actions, life-ending restrictions and phone controls. Git whitespace check passed.
