# Prototype v0.4 — People, Work, and Mzansi

## New in this version

- A rebuilt cover with a warmer South African city identity
- Three choices in every daily life situation
- A separate three-choice workplace situation every day
- Relationships with Lerato, Gogo, and Sizwe that remember decisions
- South African details including minibus taxis, the kasi economy, vetkoek trading, load shedding, ubuntu, the taxi rank, and a weekend braai
- Automatic job income with player-controlled workplace conduct
- Promotions based on experience **and** performance
- Delayed promotion when performance is too low
- Formal warnings and dismissal after repeated bad work decisions
- New career opportunities after dismissal
- Career history that prevents old careers appearing as newly discovered jobs
- One equipment purchase per career and worker hiring at leadership rank

## Suggested playtest

Play twice. In the first life, make responsible work decisions and try to earn promotions. In the second, take shortcuts until you are dismissed. Compare money, reputation, relationships, and the final life score.
