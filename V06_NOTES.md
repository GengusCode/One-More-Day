# Prototype v0.6 — From first rand to one million

## Cover

- Removed city and country labels from the artwork.
- Removed visible photo attribution from the game screen.
- Retained the real, non-AI Johannesburg photograph and preserved its CC BY-SA attribution in `V05_NOTES.md`.
- South African identity now comes from the skyline, minibus taxi, visual palette, language, people, and situations rather than a location label.

## Starting paths

Players can now begin with one of three distinct paths:

1. **Start a car wash** — progress from a bucket and roadside work to employees, permanent sites, branches, and a wash group.
2. **Open a kota stand** — grow from a home kitchen to a street stall, container kitchen, takeaway shop, and multiple locations.
3. **Join a corporate office** — climb from office junior toward executive director through performance, office politics, qualifications, and team leadership.

Each path displays progress toward R1,000,000. Automatic income grows through a one-time equipment purchase, employees or team members, and up to four major expansions.

## Longer play

Week 1 remains a milestone, but it is no longer the end of the life. The player can continue into later weeks, keep growing income, and pursue the millionaire target.

## Phone chase

Choosing to chase the phone thief now starts a touch mini-game:

- Catch the moving thief three times before time expires.
- Health and energy influence the time available.
- Winning returns the phone and improves reputation and happiness.
- Losing costs replacement money and affects health, energy, and happiness.

## Reference games

The progression presentation was informed by the readable life choices of BitLife and the small-business-to-automated-empire structure of Idle Miner Tycoon and AdVenture Capitalist. No game artwork or interface assets were copied.
